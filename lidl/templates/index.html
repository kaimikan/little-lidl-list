What's a Nutri-Score?

The A–E letter on Lidl packs

A B C D E

An EU front-of-pack label (A = green, E = red). It's a points formula scored per 100 g — and it's separate from this app's score, which reads the product name.

How it's calculated

  • − Bad points: calories, saturated fat, sugar, salt
  • + Good points: protein, fibre, % fruit / veg / legumes / nuts

Bad minus good → a letter. It's blind to frying, freezing, and processing.

Why it can mislead

  • Fried fish & oven fries score A–B — protein/fibre count, the frying doesn't.
  • Olive oil, nuts, cheese, oily fish score C–E — fat & calories are punished hard.
  • It's per 100 g, not per portion — a splash of oil looks worse than a plate of fries.

Use it for: comparing similar products (yogurt vs yogurt, bread vs bread). Don't let it override “is this a whole food?” — that's where it falls down, and where this app's protein/carb/veg lens is better.
